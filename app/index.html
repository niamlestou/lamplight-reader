Install natural-sounding voices?

Lamp Light can download the free, open-source Kokoro voices. They sound noticeably better and more natural than your computer's built-in voice, and once installed they work offline.

One-time download of about 90 MB from huggingface.co; this is the only time Lamp Light connects to the internet for voices. Your documents never leave your device. You can skip this and install later from the voice menu.
